# Caramel Dog Clicker: CrazyGames listing

## Title
Caramel Dog Clicker

## Description
Tap the caramel dog to earn treats, then turn them into a doggy empire! Hire street pals, open bakeries and pet shops, build treat factories and even a base on the Moon, all with Caramelo, the little caramel dog with big dreams.

- Tap or click the dog to collect treats, and watch him go from sitting to a full sprint as your income grows.
- Buy 27 buildings and 100+ upgrades, from a humble rubber bone to a comet that rains bones and coins.
- Catch the golden bones that drop from the sky for lucky bonuses and production frenzies.
- Ascend to Dog Heaven to earn Celestial Bones: permanent production bonuses and blessings that make every new run faster.
- Idle friendly: Caramelo keeps earning treats while you are away.

Works on desktop and mobile.

## Controls
- Mouse: click the dog to earn treats, click buildings and upgrades to buy them, scroll the list with the mouse wheel.
- Touch: tap the dog, tap to buy, drag the list to scroll.
- Keyboard: not needed.
